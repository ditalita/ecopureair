Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

Annual cleaning is commonly recommended, but the right frequency depends on dryer usage, vent length and lint buildup.

How long does a cleaning appointment take?

It varies by property and the scope of work. Request an estimate for your specific setup.

Can I request both services?

Yes, you can request an estimate for both air duct and dryer vent cleaning using the contact form below.

What affects the price?

Home size, number of vents, access, vent configuration and the extent of buildup may affect the final estimate.
